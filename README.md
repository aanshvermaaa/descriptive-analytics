## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a descriptive analytics study of Tata Motors' global vehicle sales performance from 2019 to 2025. It analyzes simulated automobile sales data to identify best-selling vehicle models, annual sales trends, regional demand, customer ratings, revenue patterns, pricing differences, and profitability.

The project demonstrates how data analytics techniques can transform raw sales records into meaningful business insights. It focuses on understanding historical performance, identifying important trends, and providing data-driven recommendations for business decision-making.

**Important:** Tata Motors' actual live transaction data is not publicly available for this project. Therefore, the analysis uses a synthetic dataset designed to represent automobile industry sales patterns. The findings are illustrative and should not be interpreted as official Tata Motors business results.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Identify the best-selling Tata Motors vehicle models by sales volume.
2. Analyze annual sales trends from 2019 to 2025.
3. Determine which year recorded the highest sales.
4. Compare regional demand across India, the UK, South Africa, and Bangladesh.
5. Analyze customer ratings by year and vehicle model.
6. Compare average on-road prices and additional costs across regions.
7. Identify the regions with the highest and lowest profit per vehicle.
8. Analyze total revenue generated by individual vehicle models.
9. Identify the model–region combinations that contribute the most revenue.
10. Develop actionable recommendations based on descriptive analytics findings.

```python
# Example: total units sold by vehicle model

      .sum()
      .sort_values(ascending=False)
```
